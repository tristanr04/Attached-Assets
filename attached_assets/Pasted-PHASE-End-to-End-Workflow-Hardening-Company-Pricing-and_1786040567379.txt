PHASE: End-to-End Workflow Hardening, Company Pricing and Template Configuration, Pole Photo Recognition, and Billing Recommendation Validation

Objective:

Make the entire Redline workflow stable, fast, mobile-friendly, and ready for controlled field testing.

The application must allow each contractor company to:

- Enter or import its own pricing
- Build its own pole and work-package templates
- Configure its own billing rules
- Configure customer-specific and contract-specific rates
- Take or upload photos of poles
- Receive suggested pole attributes from image analysis
- Match those suggested attributes to company templates
- Suggest likely billable items
- Require human confirmation before saving or billing
- Complete reports with minimal repetitive entry
- Preserve a full audit trail

The primary workflow should be:

1. Foreman opens today’s assignment.
2. Existing crew, project, equipment, and customer data are prefilled.
3. Foreman takes a photo of the pole.
4. The system analyzes the photo.
5. The system suggests likely pole characteristics.
6. The system matches the result to the company’s configured template.
7. The system loads expected work items, equipment, materials, forms, and billing codes.
8. The foreman confirms or corrects the selections.
9. The system validates for missing or inconsistent charges.
10. The report is reviewed and submitted.

Do not allow image recognition to create final billing without human review.

Do not remove, rebuild, or break existing:

- Authentication
- Company onboarding
- Company isolation
- Users and roles
- Crews
- Projects
- Work orders
- Daily reports
- Labor
- Equipment
- Materials
- Charge sheets
- Billing rules
- Work-package templates
- Custom forms
- Photos
- Signatures
- Production tracking
- Job costing
- Approvals
- Audit history
- Mobile layouts
- Existing database records

Before implementation:

1. Inspect the complete repository.
2. Review all current models, routes, forms, calculations, imports, templates, and tests.
3. Run all existing tests.
4. Record all current failures.
5. Fix broken primary workflows first.
6. Reuse existing systems instead of creating duplicates.
7. Create safe backward-compatible migrations.
8. Preserve approved reports and historical pricing.
9. Do not reset the database.
10. Do not claim a feature works unless it is tested end to end.
