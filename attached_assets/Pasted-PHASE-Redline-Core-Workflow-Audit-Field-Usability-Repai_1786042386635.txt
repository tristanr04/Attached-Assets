PHASE: Redline Core Workflow Audit, Field Usability Repair, Pricing Validation, and Backtesting Readiness

Objective:

Temporarily stop adding broad enterprise modules.

The current priority is to make Redline’s core product operate smoothly from beginning to end for an actual foreman using a phone.

The primary product promise is:

- Companies enter their own prices, charge sheets, work packages, equipment, labor classifications, and reporting templates.
- Foremen do not repeatedly enter the same information.
- Existing crew, project, equipment, pole, and billing information is reused automatically.
- Foremen can copy prior work or select saved templates.
- Foremen can photograph a pole and receive suggested pole attributes.
- The system connects confirmed pole and work information to the company’s own billing rules.
- The system identifies potentially missing or undercharged items.
- Human review is required before anything becomes final billing.
- Reports remain accurate, traceable, and isolated by company.

Do not add unrelated modules during this phase.

Do not rebuild functioning systems unnecessarily.

Do not delete or reset existing data.

2. DEFINE THE CORE FOREMAN WORKFLOW
==================================================

Build and verify this exact workflow:

1. Foreman signs in.
2. Foreman sees today’s assigned project and crew.
3. Foreman taps “Start Today’s Report.”
4. The app offers:
   - Use crew defaults
   - Copy yesterday
   - Copy a previous report
   - Use a work-package template
   - Start blank
5. Existing company, customer, contract, project, crew, and common equipment are prefilled.
6. Foreman confirms or changes crew members.
7. Foreman confirms or changes equipment.
8. Foreman selects the work performed.
9. Foreman may take or upload pole photos.
10. The system saves the photos.
11. The system may analyze the photos when a real vision provider is configured.
12. The foreman confirms pole characteristics and work performed.
13. The app loads the matching company template.
14. The app loads expected labor, equipment, materials, and billing codes.
15. Foreman changes only what was different.
16. The app detects potentially missing charges.
17. The app detects possible undercharging.
18. Foreman reviews all warnings.
19. Foreman saves or submits the report.
20. Supervisor reviews it.
21. Billing reviewer reviews pricing.
22. Approved report becomes locked.
23. Final report can be printed or exported.

Repair every point where this flow:

- Dead ends
- Requires hidden navigation
- Loses data
- Shows stale data
- Uses demo data unexpectedly
- Fails silently
- Requires unnecessary re-entry
- Breaks on mobile
- Produces inconsistent totals
