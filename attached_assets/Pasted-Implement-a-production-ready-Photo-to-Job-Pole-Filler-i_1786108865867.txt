Implement a production-ready Photo-to-Job Pole Filler in tristanr04/Attached-Assets (Redline). This is now the highest-priority foreman workflow.

Core requirement

The first thing a foreman sees after opening Redline must be the photo filler.

* Place a large, full-width Take Pole Photo button at the top of the foreman home screen, visible without scrolling.
* One tap must open the rear camera.
* Place Upload Existing Photo directly beneath it.
* Do not hide this feature inside a menu, report, tab, small icon, or multi-step workflow.
* Recent jobs and other actions should appear below the photo controls.
* Design for one-handed use at 320px, 375px, 390px, and 430px widths.

Required workflow

1. The foreman takes or uploads a pole photo.
2. Save the original photo securely before analysis so it cannot be lost.
3. Use the foreman’s company, assignments, active work, schedule, GPS, photo timestamp, mapped location, and OCR pole number/tag to find the correct job.
4. If there is one reliable match, preselect it as Suggested Job.
5. If the match is uncertain, show up to three likely company-scoped jobs for one-tap selection.
6. Never silently attach a photo to the wrong job or another company.
7. Analyze the photo and automatically populate the selected job/report with clearly marked AI-Proposed Draft values.
8. Open a review screen showing the photo beside the populated fields.
9. Require the foreman to accept, edit, or reject material proposals.
10. Require an explicit Confirm Pole Analysis action before the information becomes confirmed job data.
11. Do not allow analysis-generated information to be submitted, approved, completed, or billed before foreman confirmation.

Specific pole analysis

Return structured fields rather than a generic description:

* Pole number/tag and OCR text
* GPS, mapped location, and photo time
* Pole material, class, height, and visible condition observations
* Structure, framing, and configuration
* Circuit, voltage, and phase only when reliably supported
* Conductors and neutral arrangement
* Crossarms and braces
* Insulators
* Transformers
* Cutouts and arresters
* Switches and grounds
* Guys and anchors
* Communications attachments
* Other visible components
* Before, during, or after-work classification
* Visible installed, removed, replaced, transferred, or unchanged equipment
* Component and work quantities
* Company catalog matches
* Company work-action and work-package matches
* Required photos, forms, signatures, and documentation
* Missing evidence or additional photos required

Each field must contain:

* Proposed value
* Confidence level
* Evidence or image region supporting it
* Source: image, OCR, metadata, job context, or company catalog
* Review state: pending, accepted, edited, or rejected

When confidence is insufficient, do not guess. Ask for a specific additional image such as:

* Full-pole view
* Pole-tag close-up
* Pole-top/framing view
* Transformer or equipment close-up
* Before or after photo

Foreman confirmation

The foreman remains the final authority.

* AI may pre-fill the job but cannot finalize safety findings, specifications, work performed, quantities, materials, work units, rates, billing, or completion.
* Show low-confidence and conflicting fields prominently.
* Allow quick accept-all for high-confidence noncritical fields, while still requiring individual resolution of low-confidence or billing-relevant fields.
* Require final confirmation before company rules generate billing suggestions.
* Preserve a complete manual-entry fallback.

Company isolation and matching

Only retrieve and match against the current company’s:

* Customers and contracts
* Projects and work orders
* Pole and component catalogs
* Construction standards
* Work actions and packages
* Material kits
* Required documentation
* Effective rate versions

Never expose or suggest data belonging to another company.

Persistence and auditing

Create a versioned analysis record containing:

* Original image
* Raw structured model response
* Model and prompt version
* Confidence and evidence per field
* Original AI proposals
* Foreman accept/edit/reject decisions
* Confirmed values
* User and company IDs
* Analysis, review, and confirmation timestamps

Re-analysis must create a new version and must never silently overwrite confirmed information. Uploads, analysis retries, draft application, and confirmation must be atomic, idempotent, and concurrency-safe. Completed or locked reports must remain protected.

Preserve existing JPEG/PNG/WebP byte validation, MIME matching, size limits, private access controls, and authorization rules.

Required tests

Add tests covering:

* Photo-first foreman home layout
* Mobile camera and upload fallback
* Correct company and job matching
* Ambiguous and incorrect job matches
* OCR and field-level structured output
* Auto-filled draft persistence
* Per-field accept, edit, and reject behavior
* Required confirmation gate
* Low-confidence and additional-photo handling
* Cross-company isolation
* Foreman assignment authorization
* Completed-report locking
* Retry, idempotency, and concurrency behavior
* Re-analysis and audit history
* Fictional or de-identified pole-photo fixtures
* Field-level recognition scoring
* Mobile widths of 320px, 375px, 390px, and 430px

Acceptance criteria

A foreman should be able to open Redline, take one photo immediately, and receive a substantially pre-filled job without manually hunting through menus or searching for the report. Manual job selection should only be necessary when the match is uncertain. No AI-generated job fact or billing-relevant value becomes final without explicit foreman confirmation.

Implement working end-to-end behavior, not only mockups or architecture documents. Work on a non-main branch, add regression tests, run the full available validation suite, and open or update a draft pull request. Do not merge, deploy, push directly to main, or activate paid services.
