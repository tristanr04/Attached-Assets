PHASE: Comprehensive Pole Knowledge Base, Work Standard Library, Visual Reference Training, and Company-Specific Billing Mapping

Objective:

Build a complete company-configurable pole knowledge system that allows administrators and qualified subject-matter experts to define every pole type, framing configuration, component, work action, construction standard, required documentation item, and corresponding company billing rule.

The system must use this library when analyzing field photos.

When a foreman takes or uploads pole photos, Redline should:

1. Detect visible pole and line components.
2. Compare the photos against the company’s visual reference library.
3. Suggest the most likely pole configuration.
4. Suggest the visible work that appears to have been completed.
5. Match the result to one or more company work-package templates.
6. Load the company’s required labor, equipment, materials, forms, photos, and billing items.
7. Require foreman confirmation or correction.
8. Run missing-charge and undercharge validation.
9. Preserve the original AI suggestions and final human-approved record.

Do not assume one universal pole catalog or billing system works for every contractor, utility, territory, or contract.

Do not allow AI analysis to finalize pole specifications, quantities, work performed, or billing without authorized human approval.

Do not add unrelated enterprise modules during this phase.
